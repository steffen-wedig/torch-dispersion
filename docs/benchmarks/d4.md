# D4 benchmark (H200)

`scripts/benchmark_d4.py` on one H200 (`sbatch scripts/slurm/benchmark_d4.sbatch --oracle`):
r2SCAN-D4 at the default cutoffs (two-body 60, ATM 40, CN 30 bohr), float64, compiled kernels,
nvalchemiops neighbour lists, median of 5 runs (2 for the supercells). The D4rev parameter sets
cost the same: only the damping parameters differ.

- *E*: energy only.
- *EFS*: energy, forces and stress.
- *no ATM*: EFS with s9 = 0 (CN, EEQ and the two-body term).
- *EEQ*: the charges alone, forward and forward + backward through the solve.
- *dftd4*: the CPU oracle, dftd4 4.3.0, energy and gradient on the job's 12 cores.

- **The ATM gradient still dominates EFS**, but it no longer grows with the system size per
  tile: EFS fell 1.6–1.8× on the liquids, 3.5× at 1024 atoms and 3.4× at 2048, and peak memory
  2.5–3.7×. The per-system kernel scattered the far side's dE/dC6 with one-hot matmuls whose cost
  grew with N ([`../notes/atm_pair_gradients.md`](../notes/atm_pair_gradients.md)).
- **From 1024 to 2048 atoms EFS still grows 4.3×** for twice the triangles (energy only: 2.1×).
  The cause is not yet understood; candidates are the C6 tables (2 × 34 MB at 2048 atoms, beyond
  the L2 cache) read by the per-triangle gathers, and the dE/dC6 scatter into them.
- **Energy only is ~10 % slower** than with the per-system kernel: C6 is gathered from the
  flattened batch blocks.
- **Without ATM, D4 costs 0.02–0.03 s** per 250-atom liquid box: CN, EEQ and the two-body term.
  EEQ, including its autograd gradient, is a small part of it.
- dftd4 on 12 CPU cores takes 2.9–6.5 s per liquid box, 15–30× the GPU time with ATM.
